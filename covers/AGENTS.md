# covers/
> L2 | Parent: ../AGENTS.md

Editorial demo artwork rendered from original SVG with existing Phosphor icons and the exact CxPortal wordmark. Layered black backgrounds, white line icons and pale-green module labels follow the hierarchy observed on Caylent Insights. The rejected metal-sculpture drafts are not used. Catalog, welcome cards and social previews share these covers.

- operations/: Staffing, campaigns, case work and resiliency covers.
- governance/: Access, users, bulk actions, history and knowledge covers.
- flows/: Configuration, assistant, routing, analysis and testing covers.
- manifest.json: Module-to-public-path map for all fifteen covers.
